Default open blocks — how the free time works
Mornings carry the anchor; afternoons and evenings are deliberately blank. If you want a filler: Sillim has a big cluster of cheap food, batting cages, and jjimjilbangs within walking distance of the hotel; Line 2 from SNU reaches Hongdae / Sinchon / Gangnam in 15–30 min. Gwanaksan can also be re-run in reverse (SNU side up, Sadeokgol down) as a tempo effort.
