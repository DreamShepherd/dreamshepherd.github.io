Day 1Mon, Oct 19 · Guindy

Land, show up, then let the hotel fix you

QuantFestspaeasy evening

    free SQ 524 into MAA Terminal 2. Grab a local eSIM at the arrivals hall if you don't have data — the airport Wi-Fi is not worth your patience.ITC Grand Chola is only ~8 km from the airport in Guindy. Prepaid taxi or Uber/Ola, 20–30 min. Resist anyone offering a "tour desk" in arrivals.
  • 11:00settle
    free Check-in is 15:00, but if the room isn't ready they'll take the bags and let you use the pool and the lobby. This is a genuinely enormous hotel — an hour spent finding the pool is an hour well spent.
  • 12:30lunch
    free Keep it light. A dosa and filter coffee beats a heavy North-Indian lunch on four hours of sleep and an afternoon ahead.
  • 14:00work

  • 18:00spa
    🎟 day-of Kaya Kalp, the hotel's Ayurvedic spa. 60–90 min. Kaya Kalp. This is the correct response to a 05:00 alarm. Book when you land — evening slots go first.

    🎟 day-of Peshawri, downstairs in the hotel. North-west frontier tandoor — the dal bukhara is the reason the place exists. Walk-in works; a table for one at the kitchen counter is the better seat. In-hotel means you're asleep by 22:00.
Day 2Tue, Oct 20 · Guindy + Mylapore

Run with the deer, work, then the whole city at dusk

runQuantFest finaletemple + beach
  • 06:45run
    🎟 day-of IIT Madras campus loop, ~8 km. Spotted deer, blackbuck and macaques on the shoulder of the road — the best run in the city and it's 25 min from the bed. campus map. Needs the entry pass you grabbed yesterday. No pass? The hotel has a pool and a gym, and the Guindy / Raj Bhavan roads are flat and quiet at dawn. Either way, you're back for breakfast.
  • 08:15breakfast
    free Filter coffee, idli, a dosa. Murugan Idli Shop if you want to leave the hotel; the buffet if you don't. Both are correct.
  • 09:30work
    💼 work QuantFest finale — full day at IITM Research Park, through the awards and the closing.
  • 18:30evening
    free Marina Beach at sunset. Six kilometres of sand, the whole city out walking, and vendors selling sundal (spiced chickpeas) from baskets on their heads. Start at the lighthouse end. Don't swim. Do walk all the way to the aquarium end and back — that's the whole point.
  • 20:00dinner
    🎟 day-of Mylapore — the Kapaleeshwarar Temple tank lit up at night, then Chettinad dinner nearby. Anjappar or Hotel Ramanathan for the classic version; the temple gopuram at night is worth the detour even if you eat afterwards.
  • 21:30hotel-only
    free The alternative: skip the beach entirely, do Kaya Kalp round two, and eat at Avartana (the hotel's South-Indian tasting menu — book it today if this is the plan). A monsoon downpour is a perfectly good reason to take this branch.
Day 3Wed, Oct 21 · Mahabalipuram · then MAA

One coast day, then the 23:15 home

coastUNESCOmorning runall day yours
  • 06:45run
    free Short shakeout — hotel pool, gym, or the campus loop once more. Then pack and check out; leave the bags at the desk. Checkout is 12:00 but nobody minds you leaving luggage until the evening.
  • 08:30depart
    🎟 day-of Uber/Ola to Mahabalipuram — East Coast Road south, 55 km, 60–90 min. Call each leg when you need it, ~₹1,200–1,800 one way. Build slack into the return — pickup out on ECR takes longer than in the city.
  • 10:00anchor
    🎟 day-of Mahabalipuram: the Shore Temple (700 AD, right on the water), the Pancha Rathas (five monolithic rock-cut temples), Arjuna's Penance — the largest open-air rock relief in the world — and Krishna's Butterball, a boulder that should not be balancing and is. Ticket at the gate, ~₹40. Start at the Shore Temple and work inland; three hours covers it comfortably.
  • 13:00lunch
    🎟 day-of Seafood on ECR — a fish thali or crab fry at one of the places along the coast road south of town. Point at the tank and pick what's moving. This is the meal of the trip.
  • 15:00option
    🎟 day-of DakshinaChitra heritage museum on the way back — full-size traditional houses from all four southern states, relocated and rebuilt. ~90 min. Or skip it and swing through Mylapore if you missed the temple last night. Or go straight back and sit by the pool. All three are good.
  • 17:30hotel
    free Bags, shower, one last filter coffee at the lobby lounge. Leave for the airport by 18:45 — 20–40 min to T2.
  • 20:30airport
    free MAA T2 airside. Dinner here rather than on the plane. SQ 529 departs 23:15 → SIN 06:10 Thu.

Swaps & flex slots — the "maybe" pile
  • Theosophical Society + Adyar estuary — quiet, green, 4,000 trees, free, and the estuary walk is one of the few genuinely calm places in the city. Good if Mahabalipuram feels like too much driving on a flight day.
  • Egmore Government Museum + National Art Gallery — bronze gallery worth it on its own. A solid monsoon-proof afternoon.
  • Surfing or stand-up paddle at Covelong (Kovalam) — 30 min south on ECR, lessons in ~2 h. Swap it in for the Mahabalipuram ruins if the sea is the point rather than the temples.
  • Chepauk cricket — if there's a match at the M. A. Chidambaram Stadium, drop everything. Nothing in the city beats an Indian ground on a match night.
  • Chettinad food trail — Karaikudi is a long way, but Chettinad cooking is all over Chennai. Ameen Foods, Hotel Ramanathan, Anjappar.
  • Pondicherry — genuinely great and genuinely a separate trip. 3 hours each way. Save it.
Why this shape
Two of the three days are work, so the plan is built around the seams rather than across the middle: one spa-and-dinner landing night, one run-before-work + beach-at-dusk evening, and one full coast day before a late flight. The hotel does real work here — the Grand Chola's pool, spa and three serious restaurants mean a monsoon afternoon is a plan and not a failure. Chennai in October is the NE monsoon, which is the whole argument for keeping the hotel as a genuine branch rather than a fallback. Everything on this page is walk-in, ride-hailing, or a phone call; the only thing worth arranging in advance is Avartana, if you want it.
