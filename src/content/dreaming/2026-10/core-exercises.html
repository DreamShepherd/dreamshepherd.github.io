Core — the 5×2 set

Five rounds of 2 minutes: one minute on, one minute off — about ten minutes end to end. Default exercise is the hollow body hold — hit Refresh to roll a different five for the day.

01Today's set

Each round is one exercise. Work 60s, rest 60s, move on. Scale by reducing the hold, not by rushing reps.

02Exercise library

Bodyweight only, no equipment. Reps are a fallback — for a timed round, hold the hardest position you can control for the full 60s.

Hollow Body Hold Default

Anterior chain · anti-extension

  1. Lie on your back, arms overhead, legs straight.
  2. Press your low back flat into the floor — ribs down, no gap.
  3. Lift shoulders and legs a few inches. Legs lower = harder.
  4. Hold, breathing shallow but steady. Stop when the low back lifts.

Cue: imagine pulling your belly button toward your spine while someone pushes your ribs toward the floor.

Scale: bend knees, or hands by your hips. Raise the legs to make it easier; extend them to make it harder.

Dead Bug

Deep core · anti-extension, coordination

  1. Lie on your back, arms straight up, hips and knees at 90°.
  2. Keep the low back flat against the floor the entire time.
  3. Slowly lower the right arm overhead and the left leg toward the floor.
  4. Return, then alternate. That's one rep per side.

Cue: go half as far as you think you can — the back staying flat is the whole exercise.

Common fault: low back arching as the leg drops. Shorten the range.

Plank

Whole core · anti-extension

  1. Forearms on the floor, elbows under shoulders, feet together or apart.
  2. Squeeze glutes, tuck the pelvis slightly, pull ribs down.
  3. One straight line from head to heels. Breathe.

Cue: push the floor away with your forearms and pull your elbows toward your toes — that's the tension.

Common fault: hips sagging or piking up. If the hips drop, the set is over — reset.

Side Plank

Obliques · lateral anti-flexion

  1. Lie on one side, elbow under shoulder, forearm along the floor.
  2. Stack the feet (harder) or stagger them (easier), then lift the hips.
  3. Straight line from head through heels. Hold, then switch sides at 30s.

Cue: drive the bottom hip up toward the ceiling; don't let the top shoulder roll forward.

Scale: bottom knee on the floor.

Bird Dog

Deep core · anti-rotation, stability

  1. On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  2. Brace, then extend the right arm forward and the left leg back.
  3. Hold 2s at full extension with hips level, return, alternate.

Cue: imagine balancing a glass of water on your low back — no tipping.

Common fault: hips rotating open. Reach long, not high.

Reverse Crunch

Lower abs · posterior pelvic tilt

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet off the floor, hands by your hips.
  2. Curl the pelvis up so the tailbone lifts off the floor.
  3. Lower slowly to the start. Don't let the feet touch down.

Cue: it's a pelvic curl, not a leg swing. Knees travel toward your face.

Common fault: momentum. Slow the lowering to 3 seconds.

Lying Leg Raise

Lower abs · hip flexion

  1. Lie on your back, legs straight, hands under your hips or by your sides.
  2. Press the low back down, then lift the legs to vertical.
  3. Lower slowly — stop before the back arches.

Cue: the moment your low back lifts, stop the descent and curl back up.

Scale: bend the knees, or raise the legs less far.

Flutter Kicks

Lower abs · endurance

  1. Lie on your back, hands under the hips, legs straight.
  2. Press the low back flat and lift the heels ~6 inches.
  3. Small, fast alternating kicks. Keep the back flat throughout.

Cue: small and shallow beats hard and high — height is where the back arches.

Scale: raise the legs higher, or do single-leg holds.

Bicycle Crunch

Obliques · rotation

  1. Lie on your back, hands lightly at your temples, shoulders off the floor.
  2. Bring one knee in and rotate the opposite shoulder toward it.
  3. Switch slowly, extending the other leg. That's one rep per side.

Cue: rotate from the ribs, not the elbows. Slow beats fast here.

Common fault: pulling the neck. Keep elbows wide and hands featherlight.

Superman Hold

Posterior chain · spinal extensors

  1. Lie face down, arms overhead, legs straight.
  2. Lift arms, chest and legs a few inches off the floor.
  3. Hold, reaching long rather than cranking high.

Cue: long arms, long legs, glutes squeezed — think stretch, not crunch.

Note: this balances all the flexion work above. Good as round 5.

Bear Plank

Deep core · anti-extension under load

  1. On all fours, hands under shoulders, knees under hips.
  2. Round the back slightly, tuck the toes, lift the knees 1–2 inches.
  3. Hold — nothing moves. Hips stay level with the shoulders.

Cue: pull the floor toward you with your hands; the knees float, they don't lift.

Common fault: hips drifting up. Keep them low and still.

Hollow Rock Hard

Anterior chain · dynamic

  1. Get into a tight hollow body hold.
  2. Rock back and forth over the tailbone, keeping the shape locked.
  3. Small range — it's a moving hold, not a swing.

Cue: if the shape changes at all, go back to a static hold.

Scale: static hollow body hold, or bent knees.

V-Up Hard

Whole core · flexion

  1. Lie flat, arms overhead, legs straight.
  2. Simultaneously lift the legs and torso, reaching hands toward toes.
  3. Lower under control to the start. Don't slam down.

Cue: lead with the chest, not the arms; hinge at the hips, keep the low back safe.

Scale: tuck the knees, or do a slower hollow hold instead.

Russian Twist

Obliques · rotation

  1. Sit with knees bent, heels lightly down, torso leaned back ~45°.
  2. Hold your hands together at chest height.
  3. Rotate the ribs side to side, keeping the chest tall.

Cue: rotate the shoulders past the hips; the arms just follow.

Scale: keep the feet down and reduce the lean.

Mountain Climber

Core · conditioning

  1. Start in a high plank, hands under shoulders.
  2. Drive one knee toward the chest, then switch.
  3. Keep the hips low and the shoulders over the hands.

Cue: slow and controlled beats frantic — the hips shouldn't bounce.

Scale: step the knees in instead of driving them.

Glute Bridge March

Posterior core · anti-rotation

  1. Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat, hips lifted into a bridge.
  2. Hold the bridge and lift one knee toward your chest.
  3. Lower, alternate. Keep the hips perfectly level.

Cue: the lifted hip must not drop — that's the whole test.

Common fault: hips sagging on the switch. Slow down.

Set: 5 × 2 min (1 on / 1 off). Default is hollow body hold; Refresh rerolls five distinct exercises for the day.
